2. Privacy Policy

2.1 Data controller

The data controller is DuskTeam. You can contact us at duskidleteam@gmail.com.

2.2 Data we may collect

Depending on how you use the Bot, we may collect:

  • Discord account/identification data, such as your username and user ID.
  • Content you submit to the Bot — messages sent to AI features (e.g. /ask), files and text saved via /save-code, and media submitted to commands like /gif.
  • Moderation-related data, such as reasons given for a ban, kick, mute, or unban, and which staff member performed the action.
  • Server configuration you set up, such as channel IDs and role IDs for tickets, moderation logs, and member verification.
  • Technical data, such as activity timestamps and error logs, used to operate and troubleshoot the Bot.

Do not send passwords, card data, medical information, or other highly sensitive data unless the service and its legal basis explicitly allow it.

2.3 Purposes & legal bases

We use your data to:

  • provide, authenticate, and maintain the Bot;
  • respond to your requests and provide support;
  • prevent abuse, fraud, and security issues;
  • analyze and improve the performance of the service; and
  • comply with legal obligations.

Where applicable, the legal bases will be the performance of a contract, consent, legitimate interest, and compliance with legal obligations. We do not collect data for purposes beyond those described here.

2.4 Sharing of data

We may share data only when necessary with providers who help us operate the Bot. This currently includes:

  • Groq, to process the text you submit through the /ask command and generate a response.
  • GIPHY, to fetch a GIF image for certain automated replies. No personal data beyond the request itself is sent to GIPHY.
  • Discord Inc., as the platform the Bot operates on and through which all of the above is delivered.

We require these providers to apply protective measures and use the data only according to our instructions. We may also share information if required by law, to respond to a legal process, or to protect the rights, security, and integrity of the service, users, or third parties.

We do not sell your personal data.

2.5 Retention & security

Different data is retained for different periods, depending on what it's used for:

  • Conversation history used by /ask is kept only in the Bot's memory and is automatically cleared when the Bot restarts or after a period of inactivity. We do not keep a permanent database of these messages.
  • Server configuration for features you set up — such as ticket panels, moderation-log channels, and the verification panel — including channel IDs and role IDs, is stored persistently so the feature keeps working across restarts. This does not include your messages.
  • Ticket transcripts are generated temporarily to be uploaded to the log channel you configured; the temporary local copy is deleted immediately after upload. The transcript then exists as a message attachment inside your Discord server, governed by Discord's own retention.
  • Files saved via /save-code are stored persistently until a server admin deletes them or requests their removal.

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data, but no system is completely secure. If you detect a security issue, please inform us at duskidleteam@gmail.com.
